Overview

About this dataset

What it is, where it's from, and how it's meant to be used.

Kapuluan Atlas is a coursework data-exploration site built to visualize how the Philippines' population and vital statistics have moved since 1950 — from a young nation of 18.5 million to a projected 122.6 million by 2026, alongside the slower-moving currents underneath: falling fertility, rising life expectancy, and a steady drift from rural to urban living.

Every figure on this page traces back to a single source, cited in full at the bottom of the page. This project is independent and academic — it is not produced by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any Philippine government agency or statistical authority.

Figures for 2024–2026 include United Nations population projections rather than final census counts. Growth rate, density, and the urban/rural split are not yet available for some of these years and are marked N/A in the table below.

Glossary

Reading the columns

Each field in the dataset, grouped by what it measures, with its unit of record.

Population & settlement

Population people

Total number of Filipinos recorded for the year, at national level.

Density people / km²

Population divided by total land area — a measure of how crowded the country is on average.

Urban Population people

Number of Filipinos living in areas classified as urban.

Rural Population people

Number of Filipinos living in areas classified as rural.

Growth & mortality

Population Growth Rate % / year

Year-over-year percentage change in total population.

Live Births Per 1000 People per 1,000 people

Number of live births occurring per 1,000 people during the year.

Deaths Per 1000 People per 1,000 people

Number of deaths occurring per 1,000 people during the year, across all ages.

Infant Mortality Rate per 1,000 live births

Deaths of children under age one, per 1,000 live births in the same year.

Life & fertility

Years of Life Expectancy years, at birth

Average number of years a newborn is expected to live if current mortality patterns hold.

Births per Woman children / woman

Average number of children a woman would have over her lifetime at current age-specific birth rates.
